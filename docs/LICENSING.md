# Licensing boundaries for the published 1.4.8 reference

**Public Source Code for Transparency — All Rights Reserved.** No MIT or PolyForm
license applies to ZMetrics-owned source in this preparation. COPYRIGHT.txt and
NOTICE preserve statutory, hosting-platform and separate third-party rights.

The owner/legal copyright credit and provenance of original code, contributor
patches, five icon/logo assets and synthetic screenshot compositions require
confirmation before publication. The provisional credit is “ZMetrics contributors”;
it is not an ownership determination. Prior valid grants cannot be withdrawn
retroactively. Public GitHub terms permit viewing/forking within the platform;
reservation of rights does not override that contract or applicable law.

Independent local copying/building/execution can require authorization even though
the build is technically reproducible. The owner must decide whether to grant a
narrow auditing authorization or handle requests individually. No such grant is
implied here. Official Store end-user rights also need legal review. No general
reuse/modification/distribution/commercial license is granted.

Manrope remains SIL OFL 1.1, including its commercial permissions and conditions.
The OFL file and both font copyright notices are retained; exact binary provenance
needs confirmation. There are no bundled npm dependencies. External build tools
and Actions retain their own terms. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

CoinGecko API/data/image/trademark rights are separate. Published 1.4.8 has no
visible linked attribution or local legal pages; separately prepared attribution,
favorite/alarms and policy changes must not be presented as published 1.4.8.
Confirm current keyless distribution rights, required attribution/user terms,
and any current or future commercial plan directly with CoinGecko. Attribution
alone does not establish commercial entitlement. Do not embed a paid API key.
